     We are researching on priority-aware task scheduling mechanism to maintain critical
      house activities under ZESA Load- Shedding Schedules

**_RESEARCH QUESTION_**:

    How can an automated, priority-aware domestic task scheduling algorithm dynamically optimize household appliance runtimes to maximize the completion of critical operations and minimize grid reliance under unpredictable ZETDC load-shedding schedules?

**_WE AIM TO_**:

       Build a prototype that simulates how power is distributed according to its priority as a household task.
      
**_OBJECTIVES_**

      1. To identify and classify household activities accordance to their levels of importance based on their urgency and dependence on electricity during load- shedding times. 
      2. To design and implement a priority-aware task scheduling prototype that uses ZESA load-shedding schedules to allocate essential household activities to       available electricity periods
      3. To evaluate the performance of the scheduling model by comparing electricity utilisation under different load segments that is household, block and townships
