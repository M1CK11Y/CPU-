      house activities under ZESA Load- Shedding Schedules

**_RESEARCH QUESTION_**:

How can an automated, priority-aware domestic task scheduling algorithm dynamically optimize household appliance runtimes to maximize the completion of critical operations and minimize grid reliance under unpredictable ZETDC load-shedding schedules?

**_WE AIM TO_**:

 Build a prototype that simulates how power is distributed according to its priority as a household task.
      
**_OBJECTIVES_**
1. To identify and classify household activities accordance to their levels of importance based on their urgency and dependence on electricity during load-shedding periods.
2. To design and implement a priority-aware task scheduling prototype that uses ZESA load-shedding schedules to allocate essential household activities to available electricity periods.
3. To evaluate the performance of the scheduling model by comparing electricity utilisation under different scheduling algorithms including First-Come First-Served and Priority Scheduling.

**_DELIVERY METHOD_**

The final output of this project will be a priority-aware household task scheduling system prototype that uses ZETDC load shedding schedules to classify and grade household activities according to their level of importance.
The system will automatically prioritize critical tasks, such as food preservation, water pumping, device charging, and essential appliances, and schedule them during available electricity periods.
Using scheduling principles such as Priority Scheduling the prototype will simulate how these tasks can be allocated to available electricity periods before lower-priority activities.
By assigning priority levels and optimizing task execution under load shedding conditions, the system aims to improve the utilization of limited power availability and minimize disruptions to household operations.
